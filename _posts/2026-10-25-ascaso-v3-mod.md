## Getting to work

### Prepare your machine
Take out the water reservoir, empty it and put it aside. Than you need to take off the top plate of your Ascaso which is the cup heater. You just have to unscrew two screws at the back of your machine and than you can carefully pull the plate backwards. Be aware that there is a cable connected to the plate to heat it up. So don't just rip it off ;-) You can easily uplug the cable from the heating pad on the bottom side of the plate. Than place the top plate aside.

Now you are ready to get started.

### Giving you an overview of the components inside your machine
Be prepared to see a mess ;-) I was suprised by the density and amount of cables when I opened the machine.
